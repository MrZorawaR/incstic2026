CMT Acknowledgement

Microsoft CMT

Conference Management Toolkit

The Microsoft CMT service is used to manage the peer-review workflow for INCSTIC-2027. This secure platform supports the full submission lifecycle, from paper upload and reviewer assignment to double-blind review and final decisions. Microsoft provides this service free of charge to support scholarly conferences and research communities.

Why we use CMT

INCSTIC-2027 relies on Microsoft CMT to organize the conference process transparently and efficiently. It helps maintain a consistent review process, supports anonymity for authors and reviewers, and allows complete tracking of submissions, reviews, and decisions from the initial submission stage through final acceptance.
