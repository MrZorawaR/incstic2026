Camera Ready Submission

Instructions for the authors of accepted papers.

Authors are required to follow all the instructions listed under the General and Required categories for the final submission of their manuscript, as per the proceedings.

General Instructions

  1. Remove the header and footer (if any) in the final camera-ready manuscript.
  2. Do not include any page numbers in the camera-ready manuscript.
  3. Remove titles (Prof., Dr., Mr., Ms., etc.) before author name(s).
  4. Remove hyperlinks (if any) in the final version of the manuscript.
  5. Format the paper using the proceedings' standard template.
LaTeX Template Word (A4) Template

Guidelines

The camera-ready / final manuscript is due as per the decision notification date — see Important Dates.
