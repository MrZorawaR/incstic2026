Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to contribute to the conference by submitting original research articles that illustrate novel findings, significant projects, comprehensive surveys, or industrial experiences in the fields of Smart Technologies and Intelligent Computing. Submissions should highlight advancements, challenges and opportunities within these domains.

  • Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Computing
  • Data Science, Analytics and Knowledge Discovery
  • Computational Methods, Modelling and Optimization
  • Data-Centric Computing and Intelligent Applications

Interested authors are invited to submit full papers including results, figures and references. Papers will be accepted only by electronic submission through the Microsoft CMT link below.

Plagiarism Policy, Peer Review and Responsible Use of AI

1. Double-Blind Peer-Review Process

All manuscripts submitted to INCSTIC-2027 will undergo a double-blind peer-review process. Authors must ensure that their submitted manuscripts do not contain identifying information, including author names, affiliations, email addresses or acknowledgements that may reveal their identities. Each eligible manuscript will be evaluated by at least two independent reviewers with relevant subject expertise.

2. Plagiarism and Similarity Screening

INCSTIC-2027 considers plagiarism, self-plagiarism and duplicate publication serious violations of academic and publication ethics. All submitted manuscripts will undergo similarity screening using an appropriate plagiarism-detection tool.

Manuscripts with an overall similarity index of 15% or above will be subject to further editorial scrutiny. The similarity report will be examined to identify the nature and extent of overlapping content, including similarities arising from references, standard terminology, properly cited material and previously published work. The final decision will be based on the actual similarity report and the academic integrity of the manuscript rather than the similarity percentage alone.

Manuscripts found to contain plagiarism, substantial unattributed overlap, duplicate submissions or other forms of academic misconduct may be rejected at any stage of the review or publication process.

3. Responsible Use of Generative AI Tools

Authors may use generative AI tools for limited assistance, such as language improvement, grammar correction and proofreading, provided that such use does not compromise the originality, accuracy or integrity of the research.

However, AI tools must not be used to fabricate or manipulate research data, results, references, images or experimental findings. Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality and authenticity of all submitted content.

Any substantive use of generative AI in developing manuscript content must be transparently disclosed in accordance with the applicable publication ethics and publisher guidelines. AI tools cannot be listed as authors.

4. Editorial Decisions and Ethical Compliance

The editorial team reserves the right to reject manuscripts that do not meet the required standards of originality, research quality, ethical compliance and academic integrity. Any suspected ethical violations identified during or after the review process will be investigated appropriately.

All authors are expected to comply with the applicable publication ethics policies and guidelines of the prospective publisher. Final publication consideration will remain subject to the publisher's independent editorial evaluation and approval.

Originality and Submission Ethics

All manuscripts submitted to INCSTIC-2027 must present original, unpublished research contributions. A manuscript submitted to the conference must not have been previously published, nor must it be simultaneously under consideration by any other journal, conference or publication venue. Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions do not contain plagiarised content, duplicate publications or substantial overlap with previously published work without proper acknowledgement and citation. All sources, including previously published work by the authors themselves, must be appropriately cited.

By submitting a manuscript, authors confirm that the work is original, that all listed authors have approved the submission, and that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. Any violation of these requirements may result in rejection of the manuscript or withdrawal of its acceptance. All submissions will undergo originality screening and peer review in accordance with the conference's plagiarism policy, publication ethics and review procedures.
