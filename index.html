About the conference

About INCSTIC-2027

Welcome to INCSTIC-2027: the 1st International Conference on Next-Generation Computing, Smart Technologies, and Intelligent Computing (INCSTIC), organised by the School of Computer Science and Engineering, on 26th–27th February 2027 at Geeta University, Campus – Panipat, Haryana, India.

The conference is an annual global event designed to bring together researchers, academicians, industry leaders and technology enthusiasts from around the world. It serves as a dynamic platform for presenting and discussing groundbreaking innovations, cutting-edge research and transformative ideas in the rapidly evolving fields of smart technologies and intelligent computing.

With the increasing integration of intelligence into systems and the proliferation of smart devices, INCSTIC seeks to address the challenges and opportunities presented by this technological revolution. The conference emphasises the critical role of smart technologies and intelligent computing in shaping the future of industries, societies and economies worldwide.

INCSTIC aims to foster collaboration among researchers, academia and industry to bridge the gap between research and real-world applications. Participants will gain insights into the latest advancements in smart cities, intelligent systems, advanced networking solutions and data analytics — and the conference serves as a breeding ground for novel ideas, interdisciplinary research and innovative applications that address contemporary challenges such as sustainability, cybersecurity and user-centric design.

All the accepted and presented papers will be submitted to Publisher for possible inclusion in scopus database (Subject to approval).

Objectives

The idea of the conference is to bring scholars, scientists and industrialists from all over the world onto a common platform with the following objectives:

  • To provide a platform for presenting cutting-edge research in smart technologies and intelligent computing.
  • To explore the integration of intelligent systems in various domains, including engineering, healthcare, education and business.
  • To facilitate collaboration between academia and industry for impactful innovations.
  • To promote interdisciplinary research and address societal challenges through smart technological solutions.

Vision & Goals

To establish the International Conference on Next-Generation Computing, Smart Technologies, and Intelligent Computing (INCSTIC) as a global leader in advancing knowledge, fostering innovation, and contributing to a smarter, more sustainable and interconnected world — advancing research and innovation by fostering the presentation and exchange of groundbreaking research and innovative solutions in smart technologies and intelligent computing.

The people & place behind it

Geeta University

About Geeta University

Established on a foundation of educational excellence since 1985, Geeta University delivers a transformative learning experience that seamlessly integrates global knowledge with Indian values. Dedicated to shaping well-rounded professionals, the university focuses on cultivating innovation, ethics and a strong commitment to societal progress. Its advanced curriculum, paired with a diverse and accomplished faculty, empowers students to realise their aspirations and unlock their full potential.

The campus fosters holistic development through international exchange programs and an academic environment that balances rigorous learning with real-world application. Guided by the philosophy of Karma and symbolised by colours representing knowledge and technological advancement, Geeta University is committed to nurturing future-ready leaders. The University is established as per Section 2F of the UGC Act, 1956 vide UGC Letter No. F.No.8-3/2021 (CPP-I/PU).

About CSE

The School of Computer Science Engineering at Geeta University provides a skill set of knowledge through a practical-oriented curriculum, acknowledging the needs of the hour through industrial visits, dissertations, live project-based work, and guest lectures from national and international experts. The qualified faculty and staff work hard to impart quality education through an effective pedagogical learning process.

The School of Computer Science Engineering at Geeta University embodies a 'Centre of Excellence' that facilitates industry-oriented learning with high technical competencies.

About GTH

Geeta Technical Hub (GTH) is the training division of Geeta University, dedicated to equipping students with industry-relevant skills. It offers certification programs, hands-on projects, internships and placement opportunities that bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world corporate needs — across Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity and more.

Students gain practical experience through live projects and internships with top-tier companies. In addition to technical expertise, GTH emphasises soft skills like communication and problem-solving, and strong industry partnerships provide exclusive placement opportunities as students step into the job market.
