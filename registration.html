Registration Details

At least one of the authors of the paper has to register for the conference. It is mandatory to present the paper at the conference for inclusion in the proceedings.

CategoryIndian Authors & Delegates (INR)Foreign Authors & Delegates (USD)
Graduate Students₹8,000$200
Faculty & Research Scholars₹9,000$250
Industry₹10,500$350
Attendee Only₹1,500$100

For extra pages (more than 6), per page: ₹1,000 for Indian authors, $50 for foreign authors.

NOTE: Registration fee, once submitted, cannot be refunded.

Registration details will be shared via email along with the paper decision.
